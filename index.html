// CASE STUDY / 01 DEEP DIVE

PROFILE NEXT CAREER SERVICES

THE PROBLEM

The existing site lacked clear SEO structure, had no content pillars, and used generic jobseeker messaging that failed to appeal to experienced candidates (4+ years exp).

WHAT I FOUND

Keyword research revealed a high-intent search volume gap for executive resume writing and career transition services specifically targeting mid-to-senior professionals in India.

WHAT I CHANGED

Rebuilt the on-page SEO metadata, canonicals, and Schema.org tags. Rewrote homepage and FAQ copy end-to-end around mid-career transition pain points. Mapped a 7-pillar content calendar containing 58 search-optimized blog topics.

THE SYSTEM

Keyword Cluster → On-Page Schema → Content Pillars → Targeted Landing Page Conversion.

WHAT I LEARNED

Addressing specific reader intent directly in the headline converts far better than general promises. SEO and copywriting must be designed together from day one.
